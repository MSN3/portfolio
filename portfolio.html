about

I'm a PhD student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at Georgia Tech, working at the intersection of agentic AI and healthcare. My research spans frugal LLM diagnostic pipelines, multi-agent systems for drug discovery, and vision-language models for medical imaging — with a consistent focus on systems that are grounded, evaluated rigorously, and useful in resource-constrained settings.

Before my PhD, I completed my MS at Georgia Tech (GPA 3.95) and worked across AI agent development, sentiment/semantic modeling, and supply-chain analytics. I like building things end-to-end — from data pipelines to evaluation harnesses to the UI a domain expert actually uses.

projects
Selected research & systems work, most recent first.
Benchmarking VLMs for Hepatoblastoma PRETEXT Staging Summer 2026 (Ongoing)
Research Project · Madabhushi Lab

Built pretext_bench, a harness that converts 3D contrast-enhanced liver CT into windowed 2D panels and evaluates open-source VLMs on the 15 PRETEXT pediatric liver-tumor staging questions via an OpenAI-compatible vLLM/SGLang endpoint, with region-grounding back to voxel coordinates.

15 staging questions multi-VLM benchmark voxel-level grounding
EASE: Human-in-the-Loop Prompt Optimization for Pathology Segmentation Summer 2026
Research Project · Madabhushi Lab

TextGrad-based iterative prompt optimization for VLM-guided tumor segmentation across bladder and head-and-neck cancer, benchmarking KEEP, CONCH, and MUSK pathology foundation models with organ-aware, atlas-guided ablations.

3 foundation models 2 organ systems planned: Nature Methods
Agentic System for DIPG Drug Combination Discovery Spring 2026 (Ongoing)
Research Project · Madabhushi Lab

Multi-agent (LangGraph) pipeline with biologist, pharmacologist, and oncologist agents proposing drug combinations for DIPG from RNA-seq and a literature-grounded vector database — deployed on Slurm with open-source LLMs, with iterative critique/feedback loops between agents.

3 specialized agents open-source LLMs only Slurm-deployed
Frugal LLM for Medical Diagnosis Fall 2025 (Ongoing)
Research Project · Madabhushi Lab

A 3-phase agentic diagnostic pipeline — differential diagnosis, cost-constrained test selection, final synthesis — validated on MIMIC-IV and eICU across 10 diseases, plus a clinician reader-study app to evaluate diagnostic and test-selection quality against expert raters.

89% fewer tests 95% cost reduction planned: Nature BME
VLM Alignment for Pathology Spring 2025 – ongoing
Research Project with Dr. Anant Madabhushi

Fine-tuning an LLM via caption contrastive learning to act as a CLIP text-encoder, aligned to the pathology domain with SimCSE unsupervised training; building an interactive VLM agent for human-in-the-loop pathology slide staining.

In review for Nature journal
